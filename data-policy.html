Our Commitment

AI with Conscience

Technology should never outpace humanity. At MemoriaLife, we adhere to a strict ethical framework designed to preserve dignity, ensure privacy, and provide emotional safety.

The Purpose of This Policy

Creating a digital likeness of a human being is a profound responsibility. This document outlines the non-negotiable principles that govern our technology. We do not view our AI models as products, but as preservations of human essence that must be treated with the utmost respect and care.

Human Dignity & Respect

We prioritize the dignity of the subject above all else. Our AI is trained to reject requests that would depict the persona in a demeaning, hateful, or uncharacteristic manner. We do not create caricatures; we aim for authentic, respectful representation.

Data Ownership & Consent

You own your legacy. The final AI model and all uploaded data belong to the client.

We require explicit, verifiable consent for every persona created. For living subjects, this means direct authorization. For the deceased, we require legal proof of next-of-kin status (e.g., executor of estate). We never create "unauthorized" digital twins of public figures or private individuals.

Responsible AI Design

We employ "Human-in-the-Loop" quality checks. Before any AI persona is released, it is reviewed by a specialist to ensure it aligns with the provided personality profile and does not hallucinate harmful or historically inaccurate information. We are transparent about the limitations of AI—it is a simulation, not a resurrection.

Privacy & The Vault

Voice and biometric data are stored in isolated, AES-256 encrypted "Vaults." This data is never used to train public/foundational AI models (like generic LLMs). It stays within your private instance. We offer a "Right to Erasure" policy—if you delete your account, the AI model and all training data are permanently destroyed.

Prohibited Uses

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for the following:

Political Manipulation

Creating personas to endorse political candidates, spread propaganda, or generate fake news.

Defamation & Harassment

Using a likeness to say things the original person never would, intended to harm their reputation.

Unauthorized Minors

Processing data of individuals under 18 without strict, notarized consent from legal guardians.

Criminal Activity

Any use of the voice cloning technology for fraud, social engineering, or bypassing security systems.

Emotional Safety Guidelines

We understand that interacting with a digital memory can be overwhelming. We have built-in safeguards to protect your emotional well-being.

The "Soft Mode"

Settings to reduce the realism or intensity of the voice/avatar if the experience becomes too emotionally difficult for family members.

Clear Disclaimers

Every interaction begins with a subtle reminder that this is an AI simulation, preventing unhealthy dissociation from reality.

Grief Support

We partner with grief counselors to provide resources for families using our technology as part of their healing journey.

Continuous Evolution

AI technology evolves rapidly. We commit to quarterly ethical reviews by an independent board to ensure our policies remain at the forefront of data protection and human rights standards.

Have Ethical Concerns?

If you believe a persona has been created without consent or is being misused, please contact our dedicated Ethics & Compliance team immediately.
